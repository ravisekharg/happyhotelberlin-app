Welcome to ARO

Everything included. One simple price.

Rooms and beds by the night or by the month in Berlin-Schöneberg. Travellers passing through, students settling in for a semester, professionals on an extended assignment — ARO works for all of them.

ARO sits on Grunewaldstraße in Schöneberg, a residential street with corner bakeries, a supermarket next door, and the U7 at the end of the block. Fifteen minutes from Kurfürstendamm and KaDeWe; a neighbourhood to come home to.

Getting here

Easy to reach from anywhere

Excellent public transport links. Only about 20 km from BER Airport.

From HauptbahnhofS-Bahn + bus · AB ticket
  1. Take the S5 towards Westkreuz and get off at Zoologischer Garten.
  2. Take bus M46 towards U Britz-Süd and get off at Grunewaldstraße.
  3. Turn into Grunewaldstraße next to the Penny market. Welcome to ARO!
From BER AirportBus + U-Bahn · ABC ticket
  1. Take bus X7 towards U Rudow and get off at Rudow.
  2. Take the U7 towards Rathaus Spandau and get off at Eisenacher Straße.
  3. Use the Grunewaldstraße / Eisenacher Straße exit. We're 3 minutes' walk away.
From ZOB (central bus station)U-Bahn · AB ticket
  1. Take the U2 from Kaiserdamm towards Pankow and get off at Bismarckstraße.
  2. Change to the U7 towards Rudow and get off at Eisenacher Straße.
  3. Use the Grunewaldstraße / Eisenacher Straße exit. We're 3 minutes' walk away.

Ticket prices change. Check current fares with BVG.

FAQ

Good to know

Can't find your answer? Call us or send an email, we're happy to help.

When is the reception open?

Our reception is open daily from 7:00 to 23:00. Arriving later? Let us know in advance and we'll make sure you can get in.

Does every room have its own bathroom?

Yes. Every room, including our multi-bed rooms, has a private bathroom.

Is there WiFi?

Yes, free WiFi is available throughout the hotel, fast enough for remote work.

Can I park my car?

A parking space can be reserved for a small fee. Please request it when you book.

Do you accommodate large groups?

Absolutely. We have over 300 beds, can prepare breakfast and lunch boxes, and have two rooms your group can use exclusively. Call our group line on +49 157 53030726 or send a group request.

Can I store my luggage?

Yes, you can leave your luggage with us before check-in or after check-out.

How do I get from the airport?

From BER, take bus X7 to Rudow, then the U7 to Eisenacher Straße. We're a 3-minute walk from the station. See "Getting here" for all routes.
